I am Hitesh V S, a 2nd year Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) student at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Chennai.

My technical work spans physical hardware design, digital VLSI logic, control system modeling, and embedded robotics. I have developed autonomous aquatic waste skimmers (ALPS), approximate arithmetic adders for edge computing, dual-loop buck converter control, and precision analog irrigation controllers.

Beyond engineering, I am deeply devoted to Carnatic Music — playing the keyboard and violin, drawing, and attending live Carnatic and Bharatanatyam concerts. I am also skilled in creative media production using Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Canva, and Linux system administration.
